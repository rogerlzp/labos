## 2. Comment list

### R1 (P1) The decision record's numbering scheme is broken: D10 duplicated, D9 out of order

**Symptom** (`grep -oE "^## D[0-9]+" docs/并发调度决策记录.md`):

```
## D1 … ## D8
## D10. 三层资源模型 + 等待图死锁检测
## D11 … ## D20. 异步结果查询
## D9.  已知的未做项          ← D9 ran to the end of the document
## D10. 怎么用这份文档         ← the second D10
```

**Root cause**: when D10-D20 were inserted, the code repeatedly used `replace('## D9. 已知的未做项', new + D9)` to insert in front of D9,
so D9 was pushed further and further back; and the original section "how to use this document" happened to be called D10 as well,
colliding with the new D10.

**Why it is P1**: the document, the review package and other documents cross-reference each other with `D5` / `D10` in large
numbers. **Two D10s mean that "referring to D10" is ambiguous**; D9 being out of order means that reading by number breaks off.

**Remediation**: see "The reviewed party's response".

### R2 (P1) The correction of R1 (the banker misstatement) is incomplete, the original erroneous wording remains

**Symptom** (`暂停恢复与故障处理-设计.md:440`): the quoted line reads "**this is the real-world constraint of a non-preemptible resource** —
the theoretical banker's algorithm assumes resources are preemptible"

```
**这就是"不可抢占资源"的现实约束** —— 理论上的银行家算法假设资源可抢占，
```

**The correction section only appears at line 476** ("what was written here before … this is a misstatement").

**Why it is P1**: the review package claims "changed in 3 documents", but in reality it **added a correction note**. A reader
reading from the top **absorbs the erroneous wording first**, and the correction section is hidden 36 lines further down. **"Keeping
the original text and adding an erratum" is not the same as "fixing it"** — the erratum is for humans to read, **the document
itself must be self-consistent**.

**Remediation**: see "The reviewed party's response".

### R3 (P2) The review package claimed "D1-D20" and did not find its own structural problem

Section 3 of the review package says "the decision record D1-D20 fully recorded", but **the measurement finds 21 entries and a
broken structure**. The review package itself claims "the state was checked" (the first paragraph, "the conclusion first"), **but
it did not check the document structure**.

**Root cause**: the "quantity" was checked (1220 lines is right), the "structure" (the numbering sequence) was not. **Correct
numbers masked the structural error** — the same shape of error as the previous "the total 1860 coincidentally close to 1857".

**Remediation**: see "The reviewed party's response".

### R4 (P2) The "zero real-device verification" was self-disclosed, but no minimum real-device verification checklist was given

Section 8 of the review package lists 5 items that "the simulator cannot verify", **and that is right**. But it **does not answer
"so what does a real device have to verify, how, and how much work is it"**.

**Why it is P2**: disclosing a risk without giving a way out means that after reading it the reviewer only knows "there is a risk"
and does not know "when can the risk be eliminated". **The real-device verification checklist is the first step towards eliminating
that risk**.

**Remediation**: see "The reviewed party's response".

### R5 (P3) Two places maintain the "known not-done items"

The `D9. 已知的未做项` at the end of the decision record overlaps with the "six, known not-done items" of the review package. **The two
lists will drift apart** (one is updated, the other is forgotten).

**Remediation**: see "The reviewed party's response".
